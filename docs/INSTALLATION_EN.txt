SPIDER-MAN TURKISH-ENGLISH-DEUTSCH SETUP
========================================

INSTALLATION
------------
After installation, select Türkçe, English or Deutsch in the launcher.

Türkçe and English require a SLES-02886 BIN; Deutsch requires SLES-02888.
Select your PlayStation BIOS and the required game file will be prepared and
verified automatically. Your source BIN is not modified.

CONTROLS
--------
In DuckStation, open Settings > Controllers > Controller Port 1 >
Automatic Mapping.

Select your gamepad or keyboard there.
